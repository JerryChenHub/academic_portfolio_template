[UCI x GATI](https://www.ucigati.org/) offers summer research experiences that connect high school students with university research and mentorship at UC Irvine.

During the summers of 2025 and 2026, our [Aircraft Systems Laboratory](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/aircraftsyslab/) was selected by UCI as a host laboratory for the program. We welcomed high school students from across the United States and introduced them to engineering drawing and the research process.

## From experimental design to data

Working alongside PhD student [Melissa Lepe](https://its.uci.edu/news/spotlight-phd-student-melissa-lepe/) and [Professor Jacqueline Huynh](https://engineering.uci.edu/users/jacqueline-huynh), I guided students through planning experiments, setting up equipment, collecting data, and analyzing the results.

Our experiments focused on propeller noise under different operating conditions, using a propeller test stand I designed. I helped students connect their experimental plans to the physical setup and work through the process of making measurements and interpreting the data together.

## Sharing the joy of research

I was happy to share the enjoyment of research and engineering design with the students. Working through experiments together was a rewarding opportunity to help them explore what it feels like to ask questions, build a setup, and learn from the results.
