## The question

Can experiments with small propellers reveal noise patterns at larger scales? I am exploring this question with a test platform I designed and built, with guidance from Prof. Jacqueline Huynh in the Aircraft Systems Laboratory at UC Irvine.

## Building the experiment

The project has grown through three generations of test stands, from analog control to digital RPM control and now stepper positioning, infrared RPM sensing, and load cell measurements. The project page follows that design and learning process, from SolidWorks models to my first PCB and the first experiments.

## Work in progress

Early measurements motivate further experiments. Full automation is the next step toward testing my hypothesis about how propeller noise patterns transfer across scales.
